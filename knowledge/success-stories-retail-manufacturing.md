Retail & Manufacturing success stories (Algovia's largest sector by case-study count):

STIHL: Enterprise-Wide Agentic AI Governance Strategy — Consolidated dozens of fragmented AI initiatives into a centralized adoption plan across SAP, Databricks, and Microsoft Azure environments, with education, use-case prioritization, and a data governance strategy.

AIMS5.0 (EU Horizon programme): Edge AI and OT/IT Architecture for Real-Time Manufacturing — Placed inference close to machines for air-gapped, security-sensitive production lines, defining OT/IT bridge and sensor-fusion patterns.

Prohan: Computer Vision Defect Detection in Wood Furniture Manufacturing — Detected cracks and gaps tenths of a millimetre wide immediately after gluing, 24/7 in low light and dust. Results: 90% defect detection during production, 0% defects in final products, 0% returns from undetected flaws.

Gold Guard: Handheld Edge-AI Jewellery Authentication — Combined macro imaging, cross-polarized/coaxial lighting, IMU-gated capture, and a quantized edge-AI verification model; returns a result in under five seconds.

PNK Group: AI-Powered Defect Detection for Precast Concrete — Handheld 3D scanners and a mobile multi-camera rig detect geometric deviations and surface flaws with real-time alerts; modular system expected to cut future adaptation costs 20–30%.

ALTANA: Advanced Quality Inspection for Paint Monitoring — Colorimetric-camera computer vision for automated, real-time color-consistency checks and predictive defect flagging.

Edible Oil Producer: Automated Defective Cap Detection for High-Speed Bottling — Real-time vision system identifies cracked, loose, or missing caps on a 270-bottle-per-minute line, integrated with the existing rejection system.

10Beauty: Autonomous Manicure Innovation with Precise Computer Vision — 3D hand-positioning and nail-segmentation pipeline (accuracy greater than 0.25mm) improved efficiency by over 10x for automated manicure application.

H2TRAIN: Wearable Edge-to-Cloud Industrial Safety Platform — Integrated ECG, respiration, temperature, and movement sensors with on-device sensor fusion (TensorFlow Lite) and digital-twin cloud models for earlier warning of unsafe conditions.

Komatsu: Forest Mapping with Autonomous, Computer-Vision-Extended Drones — Drone-based 3D surface reconstruction and tree characterization (diameter, species, bend) fusing RGB camera and LiDAR data ahead of harvesting.

SatVitAi (EU Horizon Europe AGRARIAN programme, Open Call winner): Satellite Edge AI for Precision Viticulture — On-device inference pipeline for near-real-time vine maturity, disease, and yield-anomaly detection on satellite-grade edge hardware without continuous cloud connectivity.

Furniture Manufacturer: Multimodal Engineering Copilot for Drawings, Product Matching & BOMs (illustrative example, not a named client) — A copilot extracting dimensions from CAD drawings, matching them to a product library, and generating BOMs and proposal content to accelerate quotation turnaround.
