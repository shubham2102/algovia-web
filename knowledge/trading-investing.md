Algovia builds AI systems for quantitative trading firms, asset managers, hedge funds, and financial market infrastructure providers.

**Market Intelligence** — Real-time news and earnings call analysis, sentiment scoring, and event-driven signal generation. LLM-based extraction of forward guidance, management tone shifts, and regulatory filings. Latency-optimized for intraday use cases.

**Alpha Research** — ML pipelines for alternative data integration: satellite imagery, web traffic, credit card transactions, and social media signals. Feature engineering at scale with backtesting infrastructure.

**Portfolio Analytics** — AI-assisted portfolio construction, factor exposure analysis, and scenario stress testing. Natural language query interface for portfolio managers who need answers without writing SQL.

**Risk Intelligence** — Real-time VaR estimation, tail risk monitoring, and counterparty exposure aggregation. Anomaly detection on position and P&L data. Escalation workflows for limit breaches.

**Trade Execution AI** — Predictive execution analytics: optimal order routing, market impact modeling, and post-trade TCA with AI-generated commentary for compliance reporting.

**Regulatory Reporting** — Automated generation of MiFID II, EMIR, CFTC, and SEC reporting. Document understanding for interpreting regulatory changes and flagging required system updates.

Partnerships: Algovia works with specialized data providers and market infrastructure firms to deliver integrated solutions. The Lighthouse Partnership model connects clients to curated alternative data and specialized research.

Deployment: Cloud-native (AWS, GCP) and hybrid on-prem for latency-sensitive workloads. FIX protocol, kdb+, and ClickHouse integrations available.
