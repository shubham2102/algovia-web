Eureka is Algovia's enterprise AI platform — a layered architecture that connects data, reasoning, and user experience into production-ready AI systems.

**Intelligent Data Layer** — The foundation. Connectors to enterprise systems (ERP, CRM, SCADA, data lakes), real-time streaming pipelines, vector stores for semantic search, and a governed feature store. Data quality, lineage, and access controls are built in — not bolted on. Supports pgvector, Pinecone, Milvus, and Weaviate.

**Generative AI Layer** — LLM orchestration with LangGraph: multi-agent pipelines, tool bindings, RAG retrieval, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, and memory management. Model-agnostic — runs OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Llama, and domain-fine-tuned models. Streaming responses, context management, and cost controls included.

**Predictive AI Layer** — Classical and deep learning models for forecasting, classification, anomaly detection, and optimization. AutoML for rapid prototyping, full MLOps lifecycle for production. Feature store shared with the Intelligent Data Layer for consistency between training and inference.

**Dynamic UI/UX** — AI-native interface components: streaming chat, copilot sidebars, insight cards, and agentic workflow UIs. Built on Next.js with accessibility and mobile-first design. Ships with a design system that matches your brand.

**Responsible AI** — Model cards, bias auditing, explainability layers (SHAP, LIME), and governance dashboards. Audit trails for every AI decision. Compliance-ready for EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and sector-specific regulations (SAMA, FCA, HIPAA).

Eureka is available as a managed platform (hosted by Algovia), a self-hosted deployment on your cloud, or as licensed modules that integrate with your existing AI stack.
