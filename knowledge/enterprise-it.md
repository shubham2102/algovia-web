Algovia builds AI systems for enterprise IT teams — from service desk automation to AIOps at scale.

**AI Service Management** — Intelligent layer on top of existing ITSM platforms (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, BMC Remedy). Auto-classifies and routes tickets, suggests resolutions from knowledge bases, and drafts responses. Reduces L1 ticket volume by 25-40%.

**Incident and Problem Management** — AI-assisted major incident response: automatic timeline correlation, blast radius assessment, draft communications, and post-incident report generation. Root cause analysis using log clustering and change correlation.

**Change and Release Management** — Risk scoring for change requests using historical incident data and dependency graphs. Automated approval routing for low-risk changes. Release readiness checks against rollback criteria.

**IT Asset Management** — AI-assisted asset discovery, license compliance monitoring, shadow IT detection, and refresh cycle optimization. Connects to Lansweeper, Snow, and native CMDB.

**Configuration Management** — CMDB health scoring, auto-remediation of stale CIs, and relationship mapping from network scans and cloud APIs. Feeds into incident impact analysis.

**Service Desk Automation** — Conversational AI for employee self-service: password resets, software requests, onboarding workflows, and VPN troubleshooting. Integrates with Active Directory, Okta, and Slack/Teams.

**AI-Driven IT Operations (AIOps)** — Metric anomaly detection, log intelligence, and event correlation across cloud-native and on-prem environments. Reduces alert noise by 60-80% in production.

Target customers: Enterprise IT organizations with 500+ employees, managed service providers, and IT outsourcing firms.
