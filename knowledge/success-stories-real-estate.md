Real Estate success stories:

Eazli: Scalable Multi-Agent Marketplace for KSA Property, Lifestyle & Services — Eazli's early prototype could only handle simple, single-service requests, with no way to break complex needs into tasks, activate the right specialists, or give management visibility into service quality and AI costs. Algovia designed a central AI concierge that interprets each customer's needs, plans and delegates tasks to specialist agents through standardized interfaces, and shares context through RAG, with built-in monitoring of performance, quality, and per-query cost. Results: enabled new services to launch without rebuilding the core platform, cut the time and cost of integrating new specialist capabilities, opened cross-selling opportunities, and gave management real-time control over quality and AI spend. Technology: Python, LLM Orchestration, RAG, AWS.

Oculo: Turning On-Site Data into Actionable Construction Insights — Construction teams needed a single, reliable source of truth for on-site progress. Algovia's deep-tech team co-developed computer vision algorithms using 360° cameras to create an up-to-date "street view" of a construction project, delivering full-stack computer vision, frontend, backend, and mobile. Technology: OpenCV, Python, AWS, React, Kotlin, Swift.
