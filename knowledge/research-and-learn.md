Research & Learn has two parts:

Thought Leadership — a content hub for articles, points of view, and short-form insights, supported by a steady cadence of shorter pieces and LinkedIn-native posts. Content is in development; do not invent article titles or authors.

Algovia Academy — Algovia's public-facing brand for its training capability, directly extending the AI Training engagement model (see engagements) into a standing, ongoing program. Algovia Academy builds AI literacy across an entire organization — from sales and operations to support functions — moving from foundational AI literacy through hands-on practitioner skills to deeper technical tracks for teams building alongside Algovia, all grounded in real use cases. It meets board-level literacy needs, role-based team training, and technical upskilling for build engagements alike. Visitors interested in a training program can schedule a meeting via the Schedule a Meeting flow.
