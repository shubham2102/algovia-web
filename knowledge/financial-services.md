Algovia builds AI systems for banks, insurers, financial markets infrastructure, private banking, wealth management, and gaming operators navigating AML and compliance obligations.

**KYC / CDD** — AI-assisted Know Your Customer and Customer Due Diligence. Document extraction, identity verification, risk scoring, and ongoing monitoring. Reduces manual KYC time by 40-60%. Compliant with FATF, FinCEN, FCA, and MAS frameworks.

**Transaction Monitoring** — Behavioral analytics and graph models to detect suspicious transaction patterns. Precision-tuned to reduce false positives while maintaining detection coverage. Integrates with Actimize, NICE, and custom data pipelines.

**Sanctions Screening** — Real-time and batch screening against OFAC, UN, EU, and domestic sanctions lists. Fuzzy name matching with contextual disambiguation. Sub-100ms API latency for payment rails.

**Payment Fraud** — ML models for card-not-present fraud, account takeover, and first-party fraud. Ensemble approach: rules + gradient boosting + LLM-based anomaly explanation. Typically reduces fraud losses by 20-35%.

**Case Management** — AI overlay on existing case management systems (Pega, Salesforce, custom). Auto-summarizes transaction chains, suggests disposition, routes to the right analyst. Reduces average case handling time by 30-50%.

**AI Overlays** — Non-invasive AI layer on existing compliance stacks. No rip-and-replace required. Augments legacy systems with generative AI reasoning and modern data pipelines.
