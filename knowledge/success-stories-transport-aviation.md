Transport & Aviation success story:

MuuMap (D-Box): Digitizing Milk Collection Logistics Across Europe — The dairy industry relied on manual route planning and limited real-time monitoring, with poor communication between plants, farmers, and drivers. Algovia developed MuuMap, a digital system streamlining milk collection logistics through real-time tracking, optimized route planning, and seamless dispatcher-driver communication, reducing collection times and fleet requirements. Technology: Java + Spring, Kotlin, React, OpenStreetMap, Python.

Note: this is a fleet-logistics and supply-chain case study — Algovia's public-sector and enterprise engagements also extend into aviation-adjacent operational contexts as they arise, and this sector's client roster continues to grow.
