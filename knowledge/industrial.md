Algovia builds AI systems for industrial operators across oil and gas, energy, semiconductors, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, process industries, assembly, and utilities.

**Predictive Asset Intelligence** — Sensor fusion, anomaly detection, and remaining useful life (RUL) models for rotating equipment, process vessels, and electrical infrastructure. Reduces unplanned downtime by 20-40%. Integrates with OSIsoft PI, Siemens MindSphere, and OPC-UA data historians.

**Industrial LLM** — Domain-adapted language models for maintenance manuals, P&ID interpretation, incident reports, and work order management. Engineers ask questions in natural language; the system retrieves from structured documents and plant history simultaneously.

**Plant Insights** — Real-time dashboard AI that surfaces process deviations, energy efficiency opportunities, and yield optimization recommendations. Connects to DCS, SCADA, and MES systems.

**Vision AI Studio** — Computer vision for quality inspection, safety compliance monitoring, foreign object detection, and automated visual defect classification on production lines. Supports edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson and standard industrial cameras.

**Manufacturing Workflow Intelligence** — Agent-based systems for production scheduling, material handling coordination, and exception management. Human-in-the-loop for high-risk decisions; fully automated for routine reordering and shift handoffs.

Sectors: Upstream and midstream oil and gas, renewable energy operations, semiconductor fabs, API pharmaceutical manufacturing, specialty chemicals, utilities (transmission and distribution), discrete and process assembly.

Typical deployment: Edge-first architecture with cloud aggregation. Operates in air-gapped environments where required. OT/IT integration expertise across Purdue model environments.
