Algovia builds AI systems for broadcasters, streaming platforms, publishers, and content operations teams.

**Media Copilot** — Generative AI assistant for media workflows: script analysis, content tagging, rights query resolution, and broadcast rundown optimization. Reduces editorial prep time by 30-50%. Works with existing MAM, PAM, and CMS systems.

**Digital / OTT** — AI recommendation engine, content personalization, churn prediction, and ad inventory optimization for over-the-top and SVOD/AVOD platforms. Lightweight enough to run on Vercel Edge or Lambda@Edge.

**Linear Broadcasting** — Scheduling optimization, audience retention forecasting, and automated traffic management for linear TV and radio. Integrates with Imagine, Amagi, and Grass Valley playout.

**Rights and Compliance** — AI-assisted rights management: contract extraction, territory conflict detection, clearance workflow automation, and expiry monitoring. Reduces rights query resolution from days to minutes.

**Content Intelligence** — Automated metadata enrichment, scene detection, content moderation, and accessibility captioning at scale. Reduces manual QC costs by 40-60% on high-volume archives.

**Audience Intelligence** — First-party data activation, behavioral segmentation, and lookalike modeling without third-party cookies. Privacy-safe architecture compliant with GDPR and CCPA.

Audit and managed services available for media operations: compliance monitoring, content performance audits, and AI model governance reviews.
