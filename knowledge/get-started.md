Getting started with Algovia is straightforward. Three paths depending on where you are:

**Talk to Algovia AI** — Use the chat on the homepage to describe your challenge. The AI will help you articulate the problem, identify which Algovia capabilities are most relevant, and suggest next steps. No commitment required.

**Schedule a Solution Workshop** — A 90-minute structured session with Algovia's solutions team. We map your current state, identify the 2-3 highest-impact AI opportunities, and produce a written engagement outline with timeline and resource estimates. Available virtually or in person. No cost.

**Upload Your Requirements** — Have a brief, RFP, or technical spec? Upload it directly and Algovia will review and respond with a tailored approach within 2 business days.

What to expect in a first engagement:
1. Discovery workshop (1-2 weeks) — stakeholder interviews, data audit, technical assessment, opportunity prioritization
2. Engagement design (1 week) — team composition, delivery roadmap, milestones, commercial terms
3. Kick-off and sprint 1 (weeks 3-4) — foundation work: data pipelines, CI/CD, architecture alignment
4. Iterative delivery (weeks 4-14+) — biweekly demos, continuous deployment, stakeholder feedback loops
5. Production handoff — documentation, knowledge transfer, optional SLA-backed managed service

Pricing: Fixed-scope engagements from $50K for focused AI POCs. Platform licensing and team augmentation also available. All pricing discussed transparently in the Solution Workshop.

Industries where Algovia has shipped production systems: Retail/CPG, Financial Services, Industrial, Enterprise IT, Media, Trading & Investing.
