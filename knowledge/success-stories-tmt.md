Technology, Media & Telecommunications success stories:

TalkTwelve: Production Legal AI Platform — An employment-law service received open-ended client enquiries lacking key facts; solicitors spent over 60 minutes per case before a consultation could begin. Algovia built a conversational AI agent that asks targeted follow-up questions, retrieves approved legal content through RAG, and converts each enquiry into a structured, solicitor-ready brief, deployed securely on AWS. Result: cut intake time from over 60 minutes to about 12 minutes. Technology: Python, React, Qdrant, Keycloak, AWS.

Invisible Technologies: Data Platform Rebuilt for Reliability & Cost Control — A Databricks medallion architecture scaled without operational control: pipeline failures went undetected and cloud costs accumulated without attribution. Algovia built data-quality monitoring across all three medallion layers, local testing before production, hash-based deduplication, and granular cost dashboards. Technology: Python, PySpark, Databricks, Temporal, Delta Lake.

Major European Telecom Operator: Automated Fibre Optic Installation Validation — Manual inspection of fibre installations was slow and inconsistent. Algovia developed a real-time computer vision and machine learning inspection system. Technology: Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, FastAPI.

Intel Poland / Revoize: Revolutionizing In-Car Speech Enhancement — Algovia's GenAI Audio R&D initiative set new real-time audio processing standards through microphone-array geometry design and iterative DSP algorithm development, exceeding market incumbents including flagship Adobe products.
