Algovia builds AI systems for retailers and consumer goods companies across grocery, fuel, and convenience sectors.

**Shelf Planning AI** — Computer vision and demand signal fusion to optimize shelf placement, facings, and replenishment cycles. Reduces out-of-stock rates by 15-30% in production deployments. Works with existing planogram tools or replaces manual processes entirely.

**Demand Forecasting** — Multi-horizon demand forecasting using store-level sales history, weather, events, promotions, and supplier lead times. Accuracy improvement of 12-25% over legacy statistical models. Integrates with SAP, Oracle, and Blue Yonder.

**Store Operations** — AI copilots for shift managers: exception alerts, task prioritization, staffing optimization, and automated compliance checks. Reduces manager decision overhead by 2-3 hours per shift.

**Pricing Optimization** — Dynamic pricing agents that respond to competitor movements, inventory levels, and demand elasticity. Bounded by margin floors and brand guardrails.

**Supplier Intelligence** — Automated supplier performance scoring, contract compliance monitoring, and risk flagging using document RAG and structured data pipelines.

Industries served: Grocery chains, fuel retail networks, convenience store operators, CPG manufacturers, and private-label brands.
