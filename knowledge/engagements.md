Algovia has seven signature engagement models, each scoped around a business outcome:

01. AI Value Discovery Sprint — Sprint (2–3 Weeks). AI workshop including an executive diagnostic & 2–3 prioritized use cases. Outcome: a business case leadership can act on, not just an informative slide deck.

02. AI Training Sprint — Sprint (1–2 Weeks). Role-based AI training tailored to your sector and teams. Outcome: teams that can use AI, not just talk about it.

03. AI Transformation Roadmap — Strategic Engagement. Maturity diagnostic resulting in use-case prioritization, AI roadmap & change plan. Outcome: an AI roadmap tied to budget, timeline, and named owners.

04. AI Proof of Value & Solution Build — Build Engagement. AI solution design, agile build & testing, and business integration. Outcome: an AI solution live in your operating environment that scales with your business.

05. Business Transformation & Change Management — Advisory Engagement. Operating model and business process diagnostic and transformation to enable effective change management and target realization. Outcome: enhanced operating model and lean business processes that deliver tangible business value.

06. AI Governance, Risk and Compliance Management — Advisory Engagement. AI risk, governance and compliance framework and controls mapping to maintain accountability and ethical standards. Outcome: governance that regulators and boards can rely on.

07. AI Adoption & Value Realization Office — Managed Service. AI adoption monitoring, KPI dashboards, and continuous optimization. Outcome: value that is measured, sustained, and scaled long after AI solution go-live.

Every engagement starts with a conversation about the outcome the client is aiming to achieve — not a generic sales call. Visitors can schedule a meeting directly through the "Schedule a Meeting" form on the Service Offerings page, which asks for name, work email, company, job title, country/market, area of interest (the engagement model), preferred meeting format, and an optional message.
