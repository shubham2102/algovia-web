Algovia measures success by outcomes in production, not demos in a boardroom. Representative results from live deployments:

**Retail demand forecasting** — 23% reduction in forecast error for a mid-size grocery chain (800+ SKUs, 40 stores). Integrated with existing SAP supply chain. Delivered in 14 weeks from contract to production.

**Financial services KYC automation** — 55% reduction in KYC processing time for a digital bank entering three new markets. Automated document extraction and risk scoring; human review retained for flagged cases. Fully compliant with local regulatory requirements.

**Industrial predictive maintenance** — 37% reduction in unplanned downtime for a chemical processing facility. Models trained on 18 months of sensor history. Edge deployment on existing plant hardware.

**Enterprise IT service desk** — 31% reduction in L1 ticket volume for a 3,000-seat IT organization. Conversational AI handles password resets, software requests, and onboarding workflows. Integrated with ServiceNow and Active Directory.

**Media content operations** — 48% reduction in manual metadata tagging time for a broadcast archive digitization project. Vision AI + LLM pipeline processed 200,000+ hours of archive content.

**Fintech MVP** — Full-stack AI-native lending platform delivered in 12 weeks for a regulated fintech startup. Included credit scoring, document processing, and a conversational onboarding flow. Raised Series A 6 months after launch.
